Turkish Riviera & Dodecanese Islands Luxury Yacht Charter Reference Guide

Curated by YACHTMAN® Luxury Yacht Charter, this open maritime reference documents the primary cruising regions, vessel classifications, and private anchorage routes across the Eastern Mediterranean and Aegean Sea.

1. Primary Yacht Charter Regions in Türkiye

  • Bodrum Peninsula & Gulf of Gökova: Renowned for ultra-luxury motor yachts, custom wooden gulets, and direct cross-border itineraries to Kos, Leros, Patmos, and Symi. Explore Bodrum & Turkish Riviera Charter Yachts.
  • Göcek & Fethiye 12 Islands: Protected pine-fringed coves with zero road access—including Cleopatra's Bath Bay (Hamam Koyu), Tersane Island, Bedri Rahmi Bay, and Yassıca Islands. View curated Göcek & Blue Cruise Itineraries.
  • Marmaris, Hisarönü & Bozburun: Tranquil anchorages such as Bencik Fjord, Selimiye, Orhaniye, Dirsekbükü, and Datça Peninsula.
  • Kaş, Kalkan & Kekova Sunken City: Lycian coastal voyages combining ancient maritime heritage with secluded turquoise bays.

2. Crewed Charter Fleet Classifications

  • Luxury Wooden Gulets: Traditional Turkish craftsmanship combined with five-star staterooms, expansive teak decks, and dedicated private chefs.
  • Superyachts & Motor Yachts: High-performance vessels ideal for multi-island Dodecanese and Aegean voyages.
  • Modern Charter Trawlers (e.g., 20m Trawler Golden Story): Long-range stability, flybridge living spaces, and exceptional volume for family charters.
  • 50m Luxury Motor Sailer De Love: Flagship superyacht hospitality for large private groups and executive retreats.
