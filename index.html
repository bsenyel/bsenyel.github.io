Turkish Riviera & Dodecanese Islands Luxury Yacht Charter Reference Guide

Curated by YACHTMAN® Luxury Yacht Charter, this open maritime reference documents the primary cruising regions, vessel classifications, and private anchorage routes across the Eastern Mediterranean and Aegean Sea.

1. Primary Yacht Charter Regions in Türkiye

  • Bodrum Yacht Charter & Gulf of Gökova: Renowned for ultra-luxury motor yachts, custom wooden gulets, and direct cross-border itineraries to Kos, Leros, Patmos, and Symi (Bodrum Yat Kiralama).
  • Göcek Yacht Charter & Fethiye 12 Islands: Protected pine-fringed coves with zero road access—including Cleopatra's Bath Bay (Hamam Koyu), Tersane Island, Bedri Rahmi Bay, and Yassıca Islands (Göcek Tekne Kiralama).
  • Marmaris Blue Cruise, Hisarönü & Bozburun: Tranquil anchorages such as Bencik Fjord, Selimiye, Orhaniye, Dirsekbükü, and Datça Peninsula (Marmaris Mavi Tur).
  • Kaş & Kekova Yacht Charter: Lycian coastal voyages combining ancient sunken city heritage with secluded turquoise bays (Kaş Kekova Yat Kiralama).
